Quick answers

How do I approve a company?

Open Job Management and choose the Company verification tab. Press Verify or Reject on the company. Employers can only post jobs after they are verified.

Why can't students see a job post?

Students only see posts that are open. The employer must submit the post, and you must approve it under Job approvals in Job Management. The employer must also be verified.

How do I download a report?

Open Reports & Analytics, choose the dates, then press the download button of the report you want. It is saved as a CSV file that opens in Excel.

How do I add or remove a program or skill?

Use Programs & Skills on this page. A program that students already use cannot be deleted.
